## Light table coverage (menu bar icon, MenuBarIconView + SignalLight.resolve)

State    | Color          | Motion
---------|----------------|--------------------------------------------
Off      | labelColor     | opacity 1, mic.slash.fill (no colored light)
Armed    | labelColor     | 4 s sine breath, opacity 0.30-0.65
Hearing  | labelColor     | opacity 0.70 + 0.30*liveLevel, per-chunk level
Executed | systemGreen    | flash 1.0->0.55 over 0.3 s, retrigger per action
Waiting  | systemOrange   | 4 s sine breath, opacity 0.50-1.0
Error    | systemRed      | solid (mic denied / model failure)
Unsure   | not surfaced   | approved: no .unsure state in SignalLight

Priority (tested, SignalLightTests 5/5): error > loading > flash > listening > off.
