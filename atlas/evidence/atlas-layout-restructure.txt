WHY

`project.yml` inside `atlas/`. The project's own code never followed:
`tools/project_env.py` sets MODELS_DIR = <repo-root>/Models and

Models/ sit at the repo root. With the wrapper, REFLEX_MODELS pointed at a
non-existent <repo-root>/Models — the same failure class as D17.
